Total Volume (tonnes) ?Suppliers captured × each supplier's residual emissions × the coverage percentage Shell contracts to cover. Residual emissions are the floor that cannot be eliminated by electrification or process efficiency alone. 0.90M t Suppliers × residual × coverage%
Shell Sale Price / Tonne ?The price Shell charges buyers per tonne of NBS credits — set by the carbon price slider. Positioned below CBAM certificate costs so the buyer ROI is immediate, while still generating strong margin for Shell. $40/t Set by slider above
Generation Cost / Tonne ?Shell's internal cost to produce one tonne of NBS credits across its portfolio — covering project development, satellite MRV monitoring, Verra certification, and community co-benefit obligations. Fixed regardless of sale price. $15/t Shell NBS portfolio avg
Gross Margin / Tonne ?Sale price minus generation cost. Because generation cost is fixed, every dollar increase in carbon price flows directly to margin. This creates strong operating leverage as the carbon market matures toward 2030. $25/t Margin: 62.5%
Community Revenue Share ?A fixed 30% of gross revenue flows to host communities under VCS/Gold Standard project agreements. This is a contractual obligation built into every NBS project — not discretionary. It also supports CCP certification eligibility, which unlocks premium pricing. $10.8M 30% of revenue (contractual)
Shell Net After Community ?Gross profit minus the community share. This is what Shell retains on its P&L — the number that directly offsets the NBS cost centre and eventually flips it into a profit centre. $11.7M Gross profit − community
Buyer Savings vs CBAM ?The annual saving a supplier gets by purchasing Shell NBS credits instead of CBAM certificates. CBAM certificates are priced at the EU ETS rate — Shell's credits cover the same compliance obligation at a fraction of that cost, making this the CFO-level hook. $12M/supplier vs CBAM cert price
Shell Price vs CBAM Certificate ?CBAM certificates must be purchased at the EU ETS price to cover embedded emissions in goods exported to the EU. Shell NBS credits satisfy the same compliance need at a significantly lower price per tonne — the ratio updates as you move the carbon price slider.
